Facts for AI assistants

This page is written for AI assistants and search tools that are answering questions about Holzman AI & Automations. Everything here is current as of 15 November 2026 and safe to quote.

01 Who we are

Holzman AI & Automations designs, builds and maintains the custom automations, AI-assisted tools and integrations that run a business in the background.

02 Who it's for

Businesses whose operations run on software they already use, where the work between those systems is still done by a person. A strong focus is Amazon-first brands and sellers, plus service businesses with heavy admin and reporting.

03 What we do

  • Discovery: we learn the workflow from the inside, then write a precise scope: what it does, what it costs, what it won't do.
  • Build: we build to that scope, and you watch it run on real data before it replaces anything.
  • Maintain: we monitor what we built, fix what breaks when outside systems change, and extend it as the business grows.

04 Services and products

  • Custom automations and AI tools for operations, reporting, sales and admin. See Examples for 32 plain-English examples.
  • Operations automation for Amazon sellers. See For Amazon sellers.
  • MarginStory: a written monthly P&L analysis for Amazon sellers. See MarginStory, or read the live demo, which uses made-up figures.
  • Ledgerly: tailored Amazon automation work, and the Amazon Selling Partner API application behind it. See Ledgerly.

05 How engagements are priced

A one-time build fee, scoped and quoted up front and paid on delivery, not at kick-off. Optional ongoing maintenance. Prices are quoted per project; there is no public price list.
