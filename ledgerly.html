What we build

Built around how your business actually runs.

Every Amazon business runs on repetitive operations — order tracking, message handling, pricing updates, reporting, account-health monitoring. We build custom automations that handle those operations quietly in the background, so your team spends time on the work that actually needs people.

Operations & fulfillment

  • Order and inventory operations

    Syncing orders across systems, restock alerts, status notifications, and exception handling.

  • Reporting dashboards

    Consolidated views of sales, inventory, and performance pulled from your existing tools into a single place.

Growth & revenue

  • Pricing automation

    Rules-based or data-driven pricing updates, quote handling, and competitor monitoring.

  • Advertising automation

    PPC rules, bid adjustments, reporting pipelines, and alerting on spend anomalies.

Customer-facing

  • Buyer message automation

    Drafting, routing, and triaging customer messages so your team reviews and approves instead of starting from scratch.

Account integrity

  • Account health & performance monitoring

    Watching the metrics that Amazon uses to judge your account and flagging anything that moves the wrong direction.

  • Policy violation handling

    Structured processes for capturing violations, drafting responses, and keeping a paper trail.

Custom automation. If your pain point doesn't fit any of the above, describe it to us. Most of our work starts with a specific pain, not a product category.

Approach

Careful with your data and your time.

Seller data is treated as sensitive by default. Credentials are stored securely, never in plain-text scripts or spreadsheets. Every automation we build is scoped to the minimum access it needs, and we document exactly what it reads and writes.

Every automation we ship is monitored after launch. When something breaks or Amazon changes something upstream, we handle it — that's what the retainer is for.
