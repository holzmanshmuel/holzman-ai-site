How we work

Three phases. One fixed shape.

Every engagement follows the same shape — from the first conversation to long after launch. Here's what working together actually looks like.

Phase 01

Discovery

We sit with you until we understand the workflow like someone inside your business would. Then we scope the build precisely — what it does, what it costs, what it won't do.

Output: a written scope
Phase 02

Build

We build the automation against the scope and hand it over. You watch it run against real data before it replaces anything — nothing goes live until you're confident it's right.

Output: a live automation
Phase 03

Maintain

Things break. APIs change. Your business evolves. We monitor every automation we've built, fix what breaks, and extend them as your needs shift — so you can stop thinking about it.

Output: ongoing peace of mind

Who it's for

An honest fit check, before the call.

We only take on clients we can genuinely help. A quick read of both columns will tell you whether that's you — and save us both a meeting if it isn't.

You're a fit if

Likely yes
  • You know your own processes deeply, even if you aren't technical.
  • You already want automation. You're not asking "why."
  • You want someone to own the technical side so you don't have to.
  • You're okay with a short discovery before we commit to a build.

Probably not a fit if

Gracefully no
  • You need a single prompt tweaked or a one-off script run.
  • You want to hire us as staff or run an internal team.
  • You're early-stage and still figuring out what your process is.

About

My background is in building things that quietly work. I care about the part after launch far more than the part before it. The automations I'm proudest of are the ones nobody on the client's team thinks about anymore.
