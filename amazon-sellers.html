What we build for sellers

Four things that stop being someone's job.

Each of these runs today for a real seller. They are built one brand at a time, against your data and your tools, not resold as a template.

Reporting and P&L

Monthly and weekly reports that go past the numbers. They say why a number moved, in plain English, and what we would do about it next — so the report is a decision, not a spreadsheet to interpret. You can see a demo of it here: MarginStory.

Listing drafting

Drafted the way your account manager already works — their approach, their priorities, the patterns in the listings they have already written and approved — inside Amazon's limits. They review and accept in minutes instead of starting from a blank field.
