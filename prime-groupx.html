Prime GroupX bought its team’s time back.

Amazon seller operations · reimbursement documentation and invoicing, automated end to end.

A lean consulting firm ran almost entirely on skilled manual labor. We automated the repetitive core of the operation — so the team could stop doing by hand what a system does better.

01 · The firm

A lean team, a mountain of manual work.

Prime GroupX runs Amazon seller operations on behalf of its clients — approvals, reimbursements, invoicing, policy work. A high volume of detailed, recurring tasks, handled by a small team.

02 · The problem

Profitable work, done entirely by hand.

Prime’s operations ran on skilled manual labor. The work was profitable, but nearly every task was repeatable, detailed, and endless — reimbursement documentation cross-referenced by hand, invoices assembled one at a time.

Which capped the whole business: the firm could only grow as fast as the team could add hours. More clients meant more people doing the same manual work.

03 · What we built

The repetitive core, running on its own.

01

Automated

The do-it-again-tomorrow tasks — reimbursement paperwork, invoicing — now run on their own, with a human check where it matters.

02

Connected

The tools Prime already works in pass information between them automatically. No re-keying, no re-checking by hand.

03

Built to scale

As new bottlenecks appear, we automate those too — so the firm can take on more clients without adding headcount.

Same quality, a fraction of the manual hours — the team freed to focus on clients instead of paperwork.
