Sales & customers

For when leads go cold and customers wait too long for an answer.

  • Every new lead gets a reply in minutes

    An instant answer, then a follow-up a few days later, so nobody falls through the cracks.

  • WhatsApp chats saved to your customer list

    Conversations with customers are saved to your customer list on their own, so anyone on the team can see the history.

  • Quotes drafted from a few details

    Type in the job and get a ready-to-send quote or proposal in your own format.

  • Reminders for no-shows and people who never booked

    People who booked and didn't come, or got the link and never booked, get a friendly reminder.

  • Customer replies drafted for you to approve

    Answers written from the order details. Someone on your team checks them and hits send.

  • An alert when reviews repeat a complaint

    Hear about the broken part or the slow delivery before it costs you sales.

Money & admin

For the paperwork that piles up at the end of every month.

  • Invoices sent the moment a job is approved

    No more invoices waiting for someone to get around to them.

  • Polite reminders for unpaid invoices

    Late payers get chased on a schedule, so you don't have to be the bad guy.

  • Receipts pulled out of email into one sheet

    Every receipt and expense in one spreadsheet, ready for your bookkeeper.

  • Profit per product, every month

    See which products actually make money once every cost is counted.

  • Contracts filled in from a template

    Names, prices and dates go in; a clean, ready-to-sign document comes out in seconds.

  • Supplier bills checked against the order

    Overcharges and missing items get flagged before you pay.

Operations & team

For the questions, check-ins and chasing that fill your team's day.

  • A sales summary every evening

    The day's numbers arrive on WhatsApp or your team chat, without anyone putting them together.

  • Ask your data a question

    Type “how much stock do we have of X?” and get the answer. No spreadsheet digging.

  • Meeting notes turned into tasks

    Every action item goes to the right person, with a due date.

  • An inbox that sorts itself

    The important emails are flagged for you; the rest are filed where they belong.

  • Onboarding that runs itself

    A new hire's accounts, documents and first-week checklist, each step on time.

  • Shift schedules and reminders

    Staff get their schedule and a reminder before each shift, and swaps go through one place.

Content & marketing

For the writing that never gets done because something is always more urgent.

  • Product descriptions pre-drafted in your brand's voice

    Every product page written the same way, in your style, and checked by a person before it goes live.

  • Social posts from what you already have

    A finished project, a new product or a happy customer becomes a draft post.

  • Hebrew and English, both ways

    Your website, catalog and emails translated properly and kept in sync.

  • Training and demo videos from a screen recording

    Record yourself doing it once and get a clean, narrated video your team or customers can follow.
