Accessibility Statement · הצהרת נגישות

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Holzman AI & Automations is committed to making this website usable by everyone, in line with the Israeli accessibility standard (IS 5568) and WCAG 2.0 Level AA.

01 What we've done

The site is built with semantic HTML, labeled form fields, full keyboard navigability, sufficient color contrast, and no autoplaying media or flashing content.

02 Ongoing

Accessibility is reviewed as the site evolves. If you encounter anything that doesn't work well with a screen reader, keyboard, or any assistive technology, we genuinely want to know.

03 Accessibility contact (רכז נגישות)

Shmuel Holzman · shmuel@holzman.ai. We aim to respond within 3 business days.
